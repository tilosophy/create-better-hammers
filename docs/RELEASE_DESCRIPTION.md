# Create: Better Hammers

**A little workshop ingenuity, right in your hands.**

Turn ingots into sheets with four hand-built hammers, from a simple stone head to a pneumatic workshop tool. Put your material in your offhand, find a raised work surface, and strike. No stationary Mechanical Press is needed for supported sheets.

## Four tiers of practical tools

- **Stone:** iron, copper and compatible zinc sheets.
- **Iron:** everything Stone handles, plus brass and gold.
- **Diamond:** adds compatible sheets and plates from Create pressing recipes.
- **Pneumatic:** combines Diamond's material support with two-strike Sturdy Sheet crafting, Create wrench functions and backtank-powered ordinary strikes and combat.

Every hammer has a distinct 3D model, a worn wooden handle and weathered materials. Hold Shift over the item for quick instructions.

## Build a small worksite

Place stone, smooth stone or an undamaged anvil on top of the floor. Stand beside it, hold the hammer in your main hand and your material in your offhand, then right-click its top. Successful ordinary strikes consume one input and produce one sheet, with a shared one-second cooldown at normal server speed.

For Sturdy Sheets, use the Pneumatic Hammer and Powdered Obsidian on a raised magma block. Two strikes produce one Sturdy Sheet; completion applies eight durability wear, even when wearing a charged backtank.

## A tool for the workshop and the road

The Pneumatic Hammer can rotate and sneak-pick up supported Create blocks. Wearing a Create backtank with air lets ordinary sheet strikes and landed combat hits use air instead of durability, and improves the Pneumatic Hammer's attack damage and recovery speed.

## Installation and compatibility

**First public beta: 0.1.0-beta.1.** Minecraft Java **1.21.1**, **NeoForge**, and **Create 6.0.10**. Install the same hammer JAR on both client and server. This is not a Forge or Fabric build.

Development validation targets NeoForge **21.1.233** and Create **6.0.10**. The metadata accepts NeoForge 21.1.233 through the remaining 21.1 series and Create versions from 6.0.10 up to, but excluding, 6.1.0; that range is not a promise that every combination has been tested.

Zinc support requires another mod or datapack to supply a suitable pressing recipe and zinc sheet or plate. Generic sheet support uses Create's selected regular pressing recipe, which must produce exactly one guaranteed sheet or plate. It does not support every processing recipe or every addon automatically.

The approved alpha build received in-game checks for swings, textures, left-handed holding, inventory display, sheet crafting and the pneumatic seam fix. A real two-player test remains deferred for this beta.
