## Project assets

The hammer geometry is original project artwork. Material artwork was generated for this project and technically exported into the included texture swatches; the generation and export history is documented in `tools/assets/README.md`. Those notes state that no Create, Badlion or other mod texture is bundled.

The listing icon and banner were supplied and approved by the project owner on 2026-11-17. They are retained unchanged in `art/listing/`; the same icon is embedded as `logo.png`. Promotional illustrations are not screenshots of gameplay.

## Runtime dependencies

Minecraft, NeoForge, Create, Ponder, Flywheel and Registrate are separate projects governed by their respective terms and licenses. Better Hammers refers to their APIs and uses Create sound events at runtime; it does not bundle their runtime JARs or copied sound files. Install Create and its required dependencies separately.
